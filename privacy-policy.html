TEMO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how TEMO handles information when you use the website and its personal bill-tracking features, including the optional DEI electricity-bill import from Gmail.

Google data we access

Only if you choose to connect Gmail, Google asks you to authorize access. The DEI bill-sync feature requests Gmail read-only access (gmail.readonly) and basic Google account identity information. It searches for messages that appear to contain DEI electricity bills or payment confirmations and extracts relevant billing details, such as amount, due date, payment status, and message date. TEMO does not ask for your Google password.

How we use Google data

Gmail data is used only to locate and extract bill-related details for your personal bill view and history. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. TEMO does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages. We do not share Gmail data except with service providers needed to operate the feature or where legally required.

Storage and security

The integration uses a Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare KV storage to process and retain bill records and OAuth-related credentials needed for the connection. Server-side credentials are not displayed in the page. The browser session token is temporary and expires after eight hours. We use account-specific storage keys and reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Stored bill history and connection data may remain until deleted. To request deletion of stored app data and disconnection of the Gmail integration, email temo75elia@gmail.com from the account you use with TEMO and identify the Google account connected to the feature. Do not send passwords or tokens. You can also revoke TEMO's access from your Google Account's third-party connections/security settings. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not itself guarantee deletion of data already stored by TEMO.

Your choices

Connecting Gmail is optional. You may decline authorization and continue using other available TEMO features. You can withdraw Google access at any time through your Google Account settings.

Service providers and international processing

Google provides Gmail and OAuth services. Cloudflare provides infrastructure for the bill-sync feature. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and policies; processing may occur in countries where they operate.

Children

The Gmail bill-sync feature is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has connected an account, contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published here. For privacy questions or data requests, contact temo75elia@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy: TEMO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
